# FirstSign demo narration

About 303 words. Plain English. The history shown is simulated and the narration says so.

## Title

Every day, a distributor's clerk retypes hundreds of WhatsApp voice notes and photos of handwritten lists into billing. It is slow, and one wrong strength is costly. Those same orders also show illness before the hospital does. This is FirstSign.

## The chemist sends an order

A chemist sends an order the way they always do. Here, a Hindi voice note. No new app to learn, no typing. It lands in the distributor's inbox.

## Gemini reads it, a clerk approves

Gemini hears the Hindi and matches each item to the catalogue. Green means sure. This line, a children's syrup with no name, is red, so it asks the clerk. The clerk picks the right item and approves. The approval is logged, with what the model said and what the clerk changed.

## Stock radar

The same orders feed a stock radar. Diarrhoea medicine is rising in Kurla and Sion. ORS has no cover left, and chemists asked for it and could not get it. The radar says what to stock, and why.

## Health officer: alert and agent

The health officer sees the same signal on a map. A Gemini agent explains the rise, marks the cause as a hypothesis, and drafts a note and a ranked visit list for ASHA workers. A person approves. Nothing is sent before that.

## ASHA field reports

On her phone, the ASHA worker files a report in Hindi. Gemini turns it into counts, with no names. Confidence rises. A second ASHA, in another locality, reports too. Two independent reports confirm the alert.

## Back-test and environment

Is the signal early? This back-test is simulated, and labelled so. In that scenario the pharmacy signal came a median of twelve days before the official report. It also shows a false alarm and a missed event. The weather and air panel is real Open-Meteo data.

## Closing

FirstSign. Orders into bills, and a first sign of illness. Built with Gemini on Google Cloud. A real pilot is the real test.
